# Market Opportunity

## The Numbers That Matter

These are the demand signals that justify building TURN. Not aspirational TAM — actual behavioral evidence.

| Signal | Data | Source |
|--------|------|--------|
| Ghosting prevalence | 50-80% of online daters experience ghosting | Multiple studies 2023-2025 |
| Ghosting frequency | Average 4 ghostings per year for active daters | User research |
| Psychological impact | 35% of ghosted users report long-term self-doubt | Behavioral research |
| Gen Z burnout | 79% report dating app fatigue, primarily due to ghosting | Platform surveys 2025 |
| Friendship gap | 1 in 10 adults have zero close friends (up 4x from 1990) | Cigna Loneliness Index |
| Teen loneliness | WHO identifies teenagers as loneliest demographic globally | WHO 2024 |
| LinkedIn conversion | 99% of outreach messages never lead to a meeting | LinkedIn data |
| Bumble BFF conversion | Only 25% of Bumble BFF matches ever meet in real life | Platform data |
| Willingness to pay | 62% of surveyed users (n=500) would pay $10-20 to resolve conversation uncertainty | Internal survey |

## Why The Survey Number Is Weak (And What Replaces It)

The "62% would pay $10-20" number comes from a survey. Surveys overstate willingness to pay by 3-5x. Real willingness is demonstrated by actual Stripe charges.

This is why the MVP pricing strategy is $5 — cheap enough to reduce friction, but real money that demonstrates actual intent. 20 paying users in the first 2 weeks post-launch is worth more than 1000 survey respondents.

Do not use the survey number in investor conversations. Use actual conversion data instead.

## The Opportunity Size At MVP Scale

Working backward from sustainable unit economics:
- Target: 1,000 paying users at $15/conversation average (post-validation price)
- = $15,000 revenue on ~40,000 conversations (assuming 25% conversion)
- That's a small number of users in a market of 50M potential customers
- At 10,000 paying monthly subscribers at $19.99 = $200k MRR = $2.4M ARR

The total market is not the constraint. Distribution is the constraint. See `../build/implementation-plan.md` for beta launch approach.
